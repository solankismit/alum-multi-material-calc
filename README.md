# Aluminium Calculator — Feature Overview

A complete tool for aluminium window and door businesses: design windows, work out exactly how much material is needed, and turn that into a polished customer quotation — all in one place.

## 1. Designing Windows & Doors

- Build a window or door section by section — choose the track type (2-track, 3-track, or openable), whether it's all glass or glass with a mosquito mesh, how many shutters it has, and its exact height and width.
- See a live drawing of the window update instantly as you change the dimensions, so you can visually check the design before committing to it.
- Built-in checks stop you from entering impossible or unrealistic sizes, with clear messages telling you what to fix.

## 2. Automatic Material Calculation

- Instantly calculates exactly how much aluminium is needed — frame, shutter, interlock, track, and mullion pieces — for the design you've built.
- Also works out how much glass, mosquito mesh, and small hardware (like track caps and channels) is required.
- Figures out the smartest way to cut standard-length aluminium bars to get the most pieces out of them with the least material wasted, and tells you the wastage percentage.

## 3. Saving & Managing Designs (Worksheets)

- Save any calculated design as a "Worksheet" so you can come back to it later without redoing the math.
- A dashboard shows all your saved worksheets in one place for quick access.
- Print-ready reports for each worksheet, including a cutting plan (how to cut your aluminium stock) and a full windows list.
- A summary report that adds up all the profile, frame, shutter, and interlock quantities across multiple worksheets — handy for placing bulk material orders.

## 4. Building Customer Quotations

- Create a quotation straight from a saved worksheet, or manually type in sections on the spot if there's no worksheet yet.
- Similar window systems are automatically grouped so you only need to set one price for each system, saving time.
- Set your own rates for aluminium profiles, glass, mesh, and track caps.
- Add extra hardware items to the quote (like rollers, handles, or locks) with their own quantity and price — the system remembers your usual rates and suggests them automatically.
- Add labor costs the way that suits you: a flat fee, a percentage of material cost, a rate per square foot, or itemized by task (cutting, glazing, fitting, etc.).
- Add overhead costs, discounts (flat amount or percentage), your profit margin, and tax (correctly split between CGST/SGST or IGST depending on whether it's a local or out-of-state customer).
- Optionally add installation and delivery/transport charges as separate line items.
- A running total panel shows your full internal cost breakdown as you work, so you always know your numbers — this is never shown to the customer.
- Save quotations as drafts, finalize them, and track their status as they move from Draft → Sent → Accepted or Rejected, with the option to revise and resend.
- Capture customer details — name, phone, billing address, delivery address, and any reference number.

## 5. Printing & Sharing Quotations

- One-click Print or Save-as-PDF for a clean, professional, customer-ready quotation document.

## 6. Setting Up Your Business Once

- A "Rate Card" page where you set your default prices — profile rates, glass rates, hardware rates, usual labor method, overhead, profit margin, tax rate, and standard terms & conditions — so every new quotation starts pre-filled with your numbers.
- An admin area to manage the catalog of aluminium systems/profiles used throughout the calculator — add, edit, or remove them as your product range changes.

## 7. Accounts & Access

- Simple login and registration with your company details (name, phone, company info).
- A personal settings page to keep your profile and company information up to date.
- Two access levels — regular users, and Admins who can manage the shared aluminium systems catalog.

## 8. A Smooth, Reliable Experience

- Friendly loading indicators while data is fetching, so the app never feels stuck.
- Clear, friendly error screens if something goes wrong, with an easy way to recover.
- Pop-up notifications confirming when something has been saved, updated, or if there's a problem.
- Behind-the-scenes data checks that catch mistakes before they're saved, keeping your worksheets and quotations accurate.
